Privacy Policy

Rest in Him: Green Noise Sleep  ·  Last updated September 18, 2026

Rest in Him is a sleep sound app made by an independent developer. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with information. The short version: the app has no accounts, no logins, and no advertising, and it does not collect your name, your email, your location, or anything you say or record.

Information the app does not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or mailing address.
  • We do not collect or track your location.
  • We do not access your microphone, camera, photos, or contacts.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not sell or share information with advertisers or data brokers.

Information stored on your device

Your settings stay on your phone and are not sent to us. This includes your volume level, your saved sound mixes, your sleep timer preference, and whether you have dimmed the screen. If you delete the app, this information is deleted with it.

Subscriptions and purchases

Rest in Him offers an optional paid subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your credit card number, billing address, or Apple ID password.

To keep track of whether a subscription is active, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated ID that is not linked to your identity, along with basic technical details such as your device model, operating system version, country, and the record of the purchase itself. This is used only to unlock the sounds you paid for and to restore your purchase if you reinstall the app or change phones.

You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy and Apple's at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Scripture

The Bible verses shown in the app are stored inside the app itself and are drawn from the King James Version, which is in the public domain. Displaying a verse does not send any information anywhere.

Children

This app is often used by parents at a child's bedside, and it is designed with that in mind. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and it contains no advertising, no links out to social media, and no way for one user to contact another. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Health and medical

Rest in Him is a comfort and sleep aid. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition. Please use ordinary care with volume around infants, and speak with your doctor about any sleep concerns.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top. Meaningful changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or about the app in general, are welcome at restinhimapp@gmail.com.
